Example spacer — v0.1

Minimal NopSCADlib project used to qualify this archive's workflow. This is a demonstration, not an archived production model.

Parameters are in millimetres. The model is a 24 mm diameter spacer with an 8.2 mm clearance bore and 6 mm height.

For printing, place the flat face on the bed. Material and dimensional tolerances must be selected for the real application.
